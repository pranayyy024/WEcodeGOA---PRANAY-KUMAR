ACADEMIC CALENDAR & EXAMINATION POLICY 2026-2027
Document ID: ACAD-CAL-2026
Approved by: Academic Senate

1. KEY SEMESTER DATES (ODD SEMESTER 2026)
- Semester Registration & Course Add/Drop: August 1, 2026 to August 10, 2026.
- Mid-Semester Examination: October 12, 2026 to October 18, 2026.
- End-Semester Examination: December 1, 2026 to December 15, 2026.

2. EXAM ADMIT CARD & ATTENDANCE POLICY
- A minimum of 75% attendance in each lecture/laboratory course is mandatory to appear for End-Semester Examinations.
- Medical Leave Exemption: Students with severe medical emergencies can submit a verified Medical Certificate to the Academic Registrar's office within 7 days of absence for attendance condonation up to 65%.
- Admit cards can be downloaded from the Academic ERP portal 7 days before exams commence.
