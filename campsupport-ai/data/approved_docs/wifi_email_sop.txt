CAMPUS IT STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP) - WI-FI & EMAIL ACCESS
Document ID: SOP-IT-2026-001
Effective Date: July 2026

1. CAMPUS WI-FI (EduRoam & CampusNet)
- SSID: "CampusNet-Student" or "eduroam"
- Authentication: Use your official university student email (e.g., rollno@campus.edu) and password.
- Wi-Fi Troubleshooting: If authentication fails in Hostel Block A or B, verify that your 802.1x network certificate is installed. For MAC address registration of gaming consoles or IoT devices, submit an IT helpdesk request with your device MAC address.
- Support Hours: 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Monday to Saturday). Outside office hours, submit a helpdesk ticket for automatic priority routing.

2. OFFICIAL STUDENT EMAIL & PASSWORD RESET
- New students receive email activation links within 24 hours of admission confirmation.
- To reset an expired password, use the Self-Service Password Portal at https://identity.campus.edu or visit the Computer Center (Block C, Room 104) with your physical student ID card.
