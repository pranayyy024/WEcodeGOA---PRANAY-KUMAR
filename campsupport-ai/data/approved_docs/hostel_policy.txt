CAMPUS HOSTEL RULES & MAINTENANCE SOP
Document ID: HOSTEL-SOP-2026
Effective Date: July 2026

1. CURFEW & LEAVE RULES
- Regular Curfew Timing: 10:30 PM daily for all residential hostels.
- Late Entry / Night Out Permission: Must be approved by the Hostel Warden via the Campus Support Portal at least 6 hours in advance.

2. ROOM MAINTENANCE & ELECTRICAL REPAIRS
- For plumbing, carpentry, or electrical repairs in hostel rooms, submit an official Infrastructure request specifying your Hostel Block (A, B, C, or D) and Room Number.
- Emergency electrical faults are addressed 24×7 by the Campus Operations maintenance staff.
